Tic Tac Toe World

Tic-tac-toe is a simple two-player game where players take turns marking X or O on a 3x3 grid. The objective is to align three marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a classic game that has entertained players for generations. It is simple to learn but can be challenging to master. Players take turns placing their mark in one of the nine squares on the grid. The first player to align three of their marks wins the game.

Game Rules

  • Players can choose to be X or O.
  • The game is played on a 3x3 grid.
  • To win, a player must align three of their marks in a row.
  • If all squares are filled and no player has won, the game is a draw.
